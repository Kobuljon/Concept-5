AERIS ONE / CONCEPT 001
A different kind of forward.

A fictional electric grand tourer created for an educational website.
All figures below are illustrative design targets, not tested or certified specifications.

Powertrain: dual-motor electric, all-wheel drive
Power target: 400 kW / 536 hp
Acceleration target: 0–100 km/h in 3.2 seconds
Usable battery concept: 92 kWh
Range target: approximately 680 km in the Eco concept scenario
Drag coefficient target: 0.19 Cd
Charging target: 10–80% in 22 minutes, 800 V architecture
Charging assumes a compatible high-power DC charger and ideal battery conditions.
Materials concept: recycled textiles, brushed aluminum, panoramic glass

RANGE LAB ASSUMPTIONS
Eco: 13.5 kWh / 100 km
Tour: 16.8 kWh / 100 km
Sport: 21.3 kWh / 100 km
Range = 92 × (charge percent / 100) / consumption × 100
MPGe = 33.7 × 100 / 1.609344 / consumption
MPGe compares energy use; the concept uses no gasoline.
Real range would depend on speed, weather, terrain, and battery condition.

Eco marks express design intentions, not environmental certifications.
The car, brand, and specifications are fictional. Images are AI generated.
